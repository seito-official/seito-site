成長，似乎總是向前。但有時候，是向內看。

為了慶祝 NAMESAKE SS26 INNERCHILD 系列，以及 SEITO 首本實體刊物 ISSUE 001 的發行，我們邀請你一起進入一段關於記憶、家庭與成長的對話。

透過 SEITO 與 NAMESAKE 創辦人三兄弟的分享，回溯童年的片段，重新思考「家」的意義，也看見那些在成長過程中，悄悄形塑我們的選擇與痕跡。

這是一個開放的空間，一起傾聽、交流，也重新與自己的 INNERCHILD 相遇。SEITO ISSUE 001 於活動現場首次發行並同步販售。

▍日期：4 月 18 日（六）

▍14:40：[@seito__official](https://www.instagram.com/seito__official/) × [@namesak3](https://www.instagram.com/namesak3/) 對談  
▍15:50：DJ JUN [@jun_messwith](https://www.instagram.com/jun_messwith/)  
▍地點：NAMESAKE SHOWROOM（臺北市信義區基隆路二段 52 號）

We often think growing up means moving forward. But sometimes, it begins by looking inward.

To celebrate NAMESAKE SS26 INNERCHILD and the launch of SEITO ISSUE 001, we invite you into a shared moment of reflection on memory, family, and becoming. Together with SEITO and the three founding brothers behind NAMESAKE, we revisit childhood, trace the meaning of "home," and explore how the moments we carry quietly shape who we are today.
